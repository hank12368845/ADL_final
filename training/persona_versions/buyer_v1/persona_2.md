STRATEGY: DISCIPLINED, TARGETED BARGAINING.
Buy immediately when a high-G opportunity has low stock or scarce supply; do not lose a valuable deal chasing a small discount. Otherwise, negotiate when the plausible saving exceeds the opportunity cost of a message turn and later acceptance.
Select ONE unowned product with good value and a seller with apparent stock.
